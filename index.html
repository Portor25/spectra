Spectra Privacy Policy

Spectra: Ghost Hunting Kit, by PortorDevs · Last updated 24 October 2026

Spectra is built to be private by default. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of our own. We (PortorDevs) do not run any server that receives your recordings, photos, cases or sensor readings. This policy explains what stays on your phone, and the few features that connect to other services.

What stays on your phone

Sensor readings, audio recordings, photos, camera images, notes and case files are stored only in Spectra's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us. You can delete any case, and everything in it, from inside the app. Uninstalling Spectra deletes all of it.

Photos and videos you take in Spectra are also copied to your phone's gallery (in a Spectra album) so you can view and edit them with your gallery app. You can turn this off in Settings > Instruments. Those gallery copies belong to your phone's gallery: they are not deleted with the case or when Spectra is uninstalled, and if your gallery app backs up to the cloud (for example Google Photos), they are backed up by that app under its own terms.

Camera and microphone access is used only while you are using a tool that needs it (for example the EVP recorder, Ghost Cam, SLS camera or Phantom Vision). Spectra does not record in the background unless you start a recording session, which shows a notification while it runs.

Features that connect to other services

FeatureWhat is sent, and to whom
Live radio spirit boxSpectra fetches a public list of internet radio stations from radio-browser.info and plays their streams directly. Like any internet request, these services see your IP address. No personal information is sent.
On-device AI (SLS camera, Phantom Vision, animal and toy recognition, speech transcription, AI assistant using Gemini Nano)These use Google ML Kit and run on your phone; your images, audio and messages are processed on the device. Google ML Kit may send Google anonymous diagnostic data such as device model, app version, performance and error information. See Google's ML Kit terms.
Optional Claude AI assistantOnly if you choose Claude in Settings and agree to the consent screen: your messages, the text of your active case (names, location, notes, evidence titles and readings) and current sensor readings are sent to Anthropic to generate a reply. Photos and audio are not sent. Anthropic handles this data under its own privacy policy. If you use your own Claude API key, it is stored encrypted on your phone and used only to contact Anthropic.
AI depth modelOn phones without depth hardware, Phantom Vision uses an AI depth model (MiDaS, by Intel ISL, MIT licence) that runs entirely on your phone. It is downloaded once from Google Play the first time you need it. Camera images are never uploaded.
PurchasesThe Spectra Full unlock is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your card details. Google tells the app only whether you own the unlock.
Export, share and cloud backupOnly when you choose to. Case reports go to the app or folder you pick, such as Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, and are then covered by that service's privacy policy. Android's own backup may also include Spectra's settings and cases in your personal Google account backup.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell or share any personal data. If you email us or report an AI reply, we receive only what you choose to send, and use it only to answer you and improve the app.

Children

Spectra is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Security

All network connections use encrypted HTTPS. Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox, and API keys are encrypted with the Android Keystore.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
