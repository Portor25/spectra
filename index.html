Good to know

Questions.

Can Spectra prove a ghost is there?

No app can. Spectra shows you exactly what your phone's sensors measured and when, and lists the everyday causes to check, such as wiring, electronics, draughts, people and pets. What you make of the readings is up to you.

Are the readings real?

Yes. Every reading comes from your phone's own hardware. Spirit box words, Static Voice and the Talking Board only respond to real sensor events, never to a timer or a script, and each word shows the reading that caused it.

Is it free?

Yes, seven tools are free for good, including the EMF meter, spirit box word reel, EVP recorder and Haunted Places. Spectra Full is a one-off purchase that unlocks the rest, and you can try each Full tool free for 5 minutes a day first.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones and tablets running Android 10 or newer. Most phones have a magnetometer and motion sensors; light, pressure and depth sensors vary. If your phone lacks a sensor, Spectra tells you rather than faking a reading.

Does it need the internet?

Most tools work offline. The live radio spirit box and the Haunted Places map need a connection.

Where do my recordings go?

They stay on your phone, in your case files. Nothing is sent to us. You can export or back up a case when you choose. See the privacy policy.
