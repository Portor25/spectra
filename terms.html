What Spectra is

Spectra uses your phone's sensors, microphone and camera to measure and record physical conditions, for hobby investigation and entertainment. Its readings show what the phone's hardware detected. They cannot prove a paranormal cause, and ordinary things such as electronics, wiring, people, pets, draughts and sensor limits can affect them. Phone sensors are not calibrated scientific or safety instruments.

Words shown by the Spirit Box and EVP-ITC tools, and letters chosen on the Talking Board, depend on the timing and direction of sensor readings. Do not use them, or any other output from Spectra, as the basis for medical, legal, financial, safety or other important decisions.
